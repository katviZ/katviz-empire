Privacy and cookies

Katviiz AI keeps this simple.

What this site collects

  • Your answers in "Where do your hours go?" stay in your own browser (local storage) so you can come back to them. They are not sent to us unless you choose to book a call and share them.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no advertising trackers, no social-media pixels, and no forms that post your data anywhere.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. The only data kept on your device is the self-check progress described above, and a note that the opening has played, which lasts until you close the tab. You can clear the progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

When you contact us

If you email, WhatsApp or book a call, we hold what you send us only to reply and to prepare for the call. We never sell or share it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it at any time: katviz.ai5@gmail.com. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
