How the order runs

  1. Enquiry

    By hand · Phone

    It waits in someone's phone until they're free.

    On a Katviiz system · WhatsApp

    Answered on WhatsApp straight away. Size and quantity are asked, and the lead is logged.

  2. Quote

    By hand · Rate sheet + Word

    Rates copied from a sheet into Word, GST worked out, checked twice.

    On a Katviiz system · Quote engine

    Priced from your rate sheet and sent as a PDF, in minutes.

  3. Order and stock

    By hand · Phone call

    A call to the godown: do we have it?

    On a Katviiz system · Stock list

    Order confirmed, stock checked and set aside.

  4. Production

    By hand · Paper

    A paper job card, walked to the floor.

    On a Katviiz system · Job list

    The job card comes from the order, and every stage sits on one list the team sees.

  5. Dispatch

    By hand · Phone call

    The customer calls to ask where it is.

    On a Katviiz system · WhatsApp

    The customer gets a WhatsApp update when it leaves.

  6. Invoice

    By hand · Retyping

    The same numbers, typed again into Tally.

    On a Katviiz system · Tally

    The invoice goes into Tally from the order.

  7. Payment

    By hand · Memory

    It's chased when someone remembers.

    On a Katviiz system · WhatsApp

    • Paid? A thank-you goes out on WhatsApp.
    • Not yet? A reminder goes out on time.
